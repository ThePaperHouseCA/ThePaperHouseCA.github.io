My Body Thought A Straight Spine Would Be Boring

By Allyn Ment

A relatable steampunk coloring & activity book for kids with wonderfully unconventional spines-and plenty of imagination.

Explore a whimsical world of woodland animals, gears, braces, games, and adventures. Inside you'll find detailed coloring pages, creative activities, and practical pages for things like questions for your care team, personal tracking, and more.

Because having a different spine doesn't mean you have to live a different kind of childhood.
